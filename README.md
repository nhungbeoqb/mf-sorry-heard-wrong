Entry for the make.fun bounty
[Sorry, I Heard That Wrong](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-sorry-i-heard-that-wrong-1858c729):
a single line (at most 140 characters) — the apology a smart speaker gives
its owner after it mishears them.

## The line

> Sorry, I heard 'feed the dog' as 'feed the fog' — a week's kibble is
> scattered on the lawn, and the mist now waits by the back door at six.

139 characters. The speaker misheard "feed the dog" as "feed the fog", spent
a week throwing kibble at the weather — and now the fog has learned the
routine and waits by the back door like a pet.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the line to `entry.txt`
in the output directory (`/output` by default, overridable with
`OUTPUT_DIR`). Output is deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical
files.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
